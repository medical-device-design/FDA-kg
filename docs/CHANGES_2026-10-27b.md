Notes:
- NDC companion rows use the same `ndc-product/<PRODUCTID>` and `ndc-package/<code>` keys and the
  openFDA field spellings as the NDC listing, with `ex:ndcDirectoryList` = unfinished, excluded or
  compounders, so one query covers the whole NDC universe. `unfinished_package.txt` writes dates as
  `04-DEC-09`; those are typed (two-digit years below 50 are 20xx).
- The DMF list has 1,045,769 sheet rows of which 41,672 carry a DMF number (the rest are blank);
  `SUBMIT DATE` serials below Excel's 1954 floor (1940s DMFs) are converted explicitly.
- The 503B table's Contact column (a named person and phone number) is never written; the shape
  forbids `ex:contact`. The debarment and disqualification lists are statutory public lists where
  the person is the record, so names are kept as published.
- `xlsx_min.py` now streams shared strings and rows (the 52 MB DMF workbook needed 10 GB as a DOM).
