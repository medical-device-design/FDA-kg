# Change log, 2026-10-17: CBER establishment registrations built

The eBER (blood) and eHCTERS (HCT/P) public query apps were paged on October 16 to 17, 2026
(one request per second, every list and detail page saved as gzipped HTML, 105 MB, SHA-256
manifest) and parsed with `pipeline/l1_cber.py` into `out/fda-drug-biologic-kg/cber_establishments.ttl`.

| Registry | Establishments | By status | Child nodes | Triples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| eBER blood establishments | 7,577 | 3,198 active, 4,355 inactive, 24 pre-registered | 38,424 `BloodProductOperation` (product x operation marks) | 514,926 |
| eHCTERS HCT/P establishments | 5,319 | 2,438 registered, 2,859 inactive, 22 pre-registered | 58,002 `HctpFunction`, 18,183 `HctpListing`, 12,986 `HctpDonorInfo` | 631,793 |
| Total | 12,896 | | 127,595 | 1,146,719 |

riot validates, SHACL conforms (0 violations), no reporting-official or US-agent names, phones
or emails in the file (only their organization lines), 106 FEIs also appear in DECRS. Most common
blood establishment types: collection facility 2,328, plasmapheresis center 1,834, hospital blood
bank 1,145.

Two parser fixes found on the real pages and pushed: the tissue list table has unclosed rows (the
row parser now splits on `<tr`), and the blood product matrix has cells without a `<tr>` (the DOM
builder now opens one implicitly, as browsers do). Matrices are recognized only as leaf tables
with three or more columns, so the page layout table is no longer mistaken for one.

fda-drug-biologic-kg is now 37,890,388 triples; all six graphs 248,643,167.

Establishment registration coverage is now complete: DECRS (drugs and biologics through eDRLS),
eBER (blood) and eHCTERS (HCT/P). Still open: Data Dashboard (OII key), MedDRA decision, FAERS,
VAERS and SPL after the 1 TB volume, lakeFS upload when everything is complete.
